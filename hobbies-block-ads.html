Say No to Ads
Clean, Fast, and Ad-Free Browsing Experience
Why Block Ads?

Online advertising has become increasingly intrusive, tracking your behavior across websites, slowing down page loads, and consuming bandwidth. I've mastered various tools and techniques to create a cleaner, faster, and more private browsing experience.

From browser extensions to modified applications, I configure comprehensive ad-blocking solutions that work across desktop and mobile devices.

Ad-Blocking Tools I Use
uBlock Origin
Desktop Firefox Android

The gold standard of browser ad blocking. Open source, efficient, and highly configurable.

  • Wide Filter Support: EasyList, EasyPrivacy, regional lists
  • Custom Filters: Add your own blocking rules
  • Element Picker: Block specific page elements
  • Dynamic Filtering: Advanced blocking modes
  • Low Resource Usage: Minimal CPU/memory impact
  • No "Acceptable Ads": Blocks everything, no exceptions
YouTube ReVanced
Android

A modified YouTube app for Android that removes ads and adds premium features for free.

  • No Video Ads: Skip all pre-roll, mid-roll ads
  • Background Play: Listen with screen off
  • PiP Mode: Picture-in-picture support
  • SponsorBlock: Skip sponsored segments
  • Return Dislikes: See dislike counts again
  • AMOLED Theme: True black dark mode
  • HDR Support: High dynamic range video
  • Download Videos: Save for offline viewing
NewPipe
Android

A lightweight, open-source YouTube client that respects your privacy.

  • No Google Account: Watch without signing in
  • No Ads: Clean viewing experience
  • Background Play: Audio-only mode
  • Download Support: Save videos and audio
  • Popup Player: Floating video window
  • Import Subscriptions: From YouTube account
AdBlock
Desktop Safari iOS

One of the most popular ad blockers with over 65 million users. Simple to use with powerful blocking capabilities.

  • Block Ads: Remove ads from websites automatically
  • Skip Cookie Walls: Bypass annoying cookie consent popups
  • Block Distractions: Hide distracting elements on pages
  • Pause on Sites: Whitelist specific websites easily
  • Custom Filters: Add your own blocking rules
  • Malware Protection: Block malicious domains
  • Privacy Protection: Stop tracking scripts
  • YouTube Ads: Block video ads on YouTube
AdBlock vs uBlock Origin

I use AdBlock as a backup option, especially on Safari and iOS devices where uBlock Origin is not available. It's user-friendly and works well for basic ad blocking needs.

AdGuard
Windows Mac Android iOS

A powerful ad blocker that works system-wide on desktop and mobile. Blocks ads in browsers and applications.

  • System-Wide Blocking: Works in all apps, not just browsers
  • HTTPS Filtering: Blocks ads on encrypted connections
  • Custom DNS: Use AdGuard DNS for extra protection
  • Parental Control: Block adult content
  • Tracking Protection: Stop trackers and analytics
  • Stealth Mode: Hide your online footprint
  • Browser Extension: Lightweight option for browsers only
  • Mobile Apps: Available for Android and iOS
Why I Use AdGuard

AdGuard blocks ads at the system level, meaning even apps like games and social media have their ads blocked. It's my go-to solution for a completely ad-free experience across my entire device.
