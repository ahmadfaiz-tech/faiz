Study with Wayground
AI-Powered Quiz Generation for University Exams
What is Wayground?

Wayground (formerly Quizizz) is an online quiz platform that I use extensively to prepare for university exams. Instead of just passively reading notes, I create interactive quizzes that force active recall — the most effective learning technique.

My unique workflow combines AI tools like Claude Code and ChatGPT with Wayground's quiz platform to rapidly generate high-quality practice quizzes from lecture slides and past year exam papers.

My Quiz Creation Workflow
1. Gather Study Materials

Collect lecture slides (PDF/PPT) or past year exam papers for the subject I'm studying. These become the source material for my quizzes.

2. AI Quiz Generation

Ask Claude Code or ChatGPT to analyze the materials and generate quiz questions. The AI creates multiple-choice questions, fill-in-the-blanks, and more based on key concepts.

3. Generate VBA Format

Request the AI to output the questions in VBA macro format. This allows bulk importing into Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx → .xlsm) with proper formatting.

4. Upload to Wayground

Use Wayground's "Import questions from spreadsheet" feature to upload the Excel file. Instantly creates a complete quiz with all questions formatted correctly.

5. Practice & Share

Practice the quiz repeatedly until mastery. Share completed quizzes with classmates — helping everyone succeed together without charging anyone!

Why This Method Works
Science-Backed Learning
  • Active Recall: Quizzes force your brain to retrieve information, strengthening memory
  • Spaced Repetition: Practice quizzes multiple times over days/weeks
  • Instant Feedback: Know immediately what needs more work
  • AI Accuracy: AI extracts key concepts humans might miss
  • Time Efficient: Generate 100+ questions in minutes vs hours manually
  • Community Learning: Shared quizzes benefit everyone
